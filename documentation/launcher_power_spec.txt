--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MECHANICAL COMPARTMENT CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Shell Mechanism: 3D-printed/molded external protective cartridge casing.
* Interface Terminals: Gold-plated spring-loaded receiver contacts inside launcher 
  bay matching flat exposed copper traces on the battery cartridge nose.
* Retention: Spring-loaded mechanical thumb-latch locking wedge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SYSTEM TETHERED INTERCONNECT SPECIFICATION (CROSS-TABLE POWER POOL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Interface Port: 2-Pin Magnetic Breakaway or Dedicated Auxiliary USB-C Interface
* Interconnect Bus Topology: Direct Parallel 5V VCC / System Ground Bridging Link
* Pin 1: Shared 5V VCC Rail (Tethers Cap Dock Boost Output directly to Launcher Bus)
* Pin 2: System Common Ground (Establishes static electrical zero-reference baseline)

Operational Behavior:
When the physical cross-table tether cable is engaged, Unit 1 (Launcher) and Unit 2 
(Smart Mat Cap) merge into a single 13,000 mAh power network. Independent voltage 
monitoring loops automatically balance the total system drain, enabling up to 37.4 
hours of unified, continuous portable dueling runtime.
